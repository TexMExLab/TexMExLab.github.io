Experimental Cosmology · UT Austin

From the the first light to the first stars with millimeter experiments.

We build and deploy cryogenic telescopes and polarimeters, from the high Atacama desert to the stratosphere over Antarctica, to measure the cosmic microwave background and the polarized glow of Galactic dust.

What we do

CMB

Simons Observatory

Small and large aperture telescopes in Chile mapping CMB temperature and polarization to probe inflation, neutrinos and dark energy.

 Balloon

BLAST Telescopes

Balloon-borne submillimeter polarimetry that traces magnetic fields threading Milky Way dust clouds.

 Instrumentation

Cryogenics & Detectors

Sub-Kelvin cryostats, readout, optics and calibration. The hardware behind the science.

 Public

Outreach

Public talks, science fair demos and observing nights bringing cosmology to Austin and beyond.
