Outreach & Engagement

Cosmology for everyone.

We share the excitement of exploring the universe through public talks, science fair demonstrations, panels and public observing nights.

What we offer

Talks

Public talks & panels

Accessible presentations on the CMB, the Big Bang and how we build telescopes for extreme environments.

Demos

Science fair demos

Hands-on exhibits of cryogenics, microwaves and detectors for K-12 students and families.

Observing

Public observing nights

Telescopes out, sky open: come look at planets, nebulae and galaxies with us.

Mentoring

Student training

Summer-school materials and open notebooks to help new students learn CMB analysis.
